# Donor Chart: Client Guide

Everything happens in the ESC Decision Matrix Google Sheet and the chart link. There is nothing to install.

## Add or update a donor

1. Open the **Donor Score Input** tab.
2. Type the donor's name in the next empty row of the **Donor** column.
3. Score each of the five criteria **-1, 0, or 1**. The **Rating Guide** tab explains what each score means.
4. Persona, Total Score, and Tier Rank fill in automatically.

To remove a donor, delete their row.

## View the chart

1. Click **Open the donor chart** on the How to Use tab (or use the bookmarked link).
2. Wait a second or two while it loads the latest data from the sheet.
3. Hover over a point, or a name in the **Priority Order** list, to see that donor's profile: their quadrant, persona, and how they scored on each criterion.
4. Click a point or a name to keep the profile open. Close it with the × button, the Esc key, or a click on an empty part of the chart.

If the chart is already open after you edit the sheet, click **Refresh data**.

## Reading the chart

| | Low effort (left) | High effort (right) |
|---|---|---|
| **High impact (top)** | **Quick Wins:** pursue first | **Strategic Investments:** worth it, plan for the work |
| **Low impact (bottom)** | **Low Priority:** easy but small | **Reconsider Investment:** hard and small |

Points are colored by quadrant (green, blue, gray, amber), and light gray points sit exactly on a dividing line. The chips at the top count the donors in each quadrant. The **Priority Order** list ranks every donor: Quick Wins first, then Strategic Investments, borderline donors, Low Priority, and Reconsider, with higher impact ranked first within each group.

In a profile, each criterion shows three squares: red for negative, gray for neutral, green for positive. The lit square is the donor's score.

Impact weighs past giving most, then free market alignment, then ESC affiliation. Effort is lower the more a donor has given before and the more ties they have to the team, ESC, and UMD.

## Change what matters most

The **Chart Settings** tab holds a weight for each criterion. A higher number makes that criterion move donors further; 0 means it is not used on that axis. Change a number, then click **Refresh data** on the chart.

## If something looks wrong

- **A "data warning" note under the status line:** click it. It names the donor and the cell to fix, such as a blank score.
- **A red error message:** most often a column header on Donor Score Input was renamed. Change it back to exactly what it was.
- **Do not rename** the Donor Score Input or Chart Settings tabs, or the column headers on them.

For anything else, contact the person who set up the chart.
